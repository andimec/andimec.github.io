## Scope

Acquisition and transfer of technical knowledge on the ECR ion source through experimental activities, system requirements review, magnetic simulations, and hands-on experience in the operation and maintenance of the LIPAc injector.

## Main tasks

- Participation in extended stays for the commissioning, maintenance, and operation of the LIPAc accelerator in Japan.
- Review and update of Injector Control Systems, including the identification of obsolete components and technologies and the definition of potential upgrade actions.
- Preparation of experimental campaigns to improve the long-term lifetime of the boron nitride (BN) disk.
- Magnetic simulation using FEMM of coils in the plasma chamber.
- Control and data-acquisition engineering using **EPICS, Phoebus and LabVIEW**, connecting injector hardware with monitoring, control and experimental workflows.
- Development of digital-engineering concepts for accelerator-system representation, testing and operator-training applications.

## ECR ion-source lifetime study

A current R&D activity focuses on extending the lifetime of the BN disk used inside the ECR ion source.

The experimental approach investigates a modified magnetic-field configuration in which the ECR region is shifted away from the BN disk on the RF-injection side. The submitted study reports that nominal beam performance could be maintained without significant BN-disk erosion, while CW operation was reached more easily and the stability of the pulsed-beam flat-top was improved.

Further validation is planned through long-duration operation.

Authors: Tomoya Akagi, Anderson Sabogal, Pau Gonzalez, Christophe Vermare, Fabio Cismondi, Benoit Bolzon, Nicolas Chauvin and Ivan Podadera.

## Controls and digital engineering

Control and digital-engineering activities are treated as part of the injector engineering scope rather than as a separate project. They include:

- EPICS/Phoebus control and monitoring.
- LabVIEW-based data acquisition and experimental interfaces.
- Integration of physical injector systems with software-based control.
- Digital representations of accelerator systems for testing and operator training.
- Data-driven and advanced-control concepts where relevant to injector and experimental-system development.
