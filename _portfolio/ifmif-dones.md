## Objective

**IFMIF-DONES** is a major fusion research infrastructure designed to qualify materials for future fusion power plants.

My work focuses on the interface between **accelerator engineering, vacuum technology, safety engineering and experimental research**.

## Engineering scope

- Accelerator vacuum systems
- Vacuum-loss accident scenarios
- Safety and mitigation strategies
- Vacuum instrumentation and diagnostics
- Fast isolation systems
- Differential vacuum concepts
- Experimental validation
- System integration and testing
- Engineering documentation and technical coordination

## My contribution

My activities connect safety analysis with physical experimentation and engineering modelling.

The work includes:

- Analysis of vacuum-system behaviour during abnormal events
- Development and experimental validation of mitigation strategies
- Definition and execution of experimental campaigns
- Vacuum-system modelling
- Instrumentation and control integration
- Technical documentation and reporting
- Coordination across mechanical, vacuum, controls and safety interfaces
- Contribution to international technical collaboration

## Engineering approach

The work follows a systems-engineering cycle:

**Requirements → Architecture → Analysis → Design → Simulation → Integration → Testing → Validation**

This approach is particularly important for accelerator systems, where vacuum performance, machine protection, safety and experimental requirements are strongly coupled.
