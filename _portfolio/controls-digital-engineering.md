## Overview

My engineering work increasingly combines physical systems with software-based control, data acquisition and digital modelling.

## Technologies

- EPICS
- Phoebus
- LabVIEW
- Python
- MATLAB / Octave
- Data acquisition and experimental analysis
- Digital-twin concepts
- Machine-learning-assisted control research

## Research direction

A current research direction is the combination of experimental vacuum systems, EPICS-based control, surrogate modelling and reinforcement learning.

The objective is to move from isolated simulations toward **validated digital representations of physical experimental systems** that can support testing, operator training and advanced control research.
