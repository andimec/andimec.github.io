## Motivation

The Fusion Student Challenge was created to connect university education with practical engineering problems in fusion technology.

The initiative was designed to help students move from theoretical concepts toward real engineering activities, including requirements definition, CAD, vacuum technology, manufacturing and experimental work.

## Technical project. Farnsworth fusor

The first practical activity was the conceptual design and construction of a Farnsworth fusor.

## Outcome

The project explored how an engineering challenge can be structured as a multidisciplinary learning experience, combining fusion concepts, mechanical design, vacuum technology, manufacturing and teamwork.
