## Overview

The **Fusion Student Challenge** is an educational concept developed to connect university students with practical challenges inspired by fusion-energy research.

The initiative is designed around multidisciplinary teamwork, hands-on engineering and the translation of academic knowledge into realistic technical problems.

## Objectives

- Encourage interdisciplinary collaboration between engineering and physics students
- Connect academic learning with practical fusion-technology challenges
- Promote engineering design, modelling and experimentation
- Create opportunities for students to engage with the wider fusion community

## Engineering perspective

The concept reflects my interest in combining technical education with practical engineering. It builds on experience from accelerator technology, experimental engineering and multidisciplinary student projects.
