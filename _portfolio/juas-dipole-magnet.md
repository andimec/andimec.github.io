## Overview

This project was developed as part of the **Joint Universities Accelerator School (JUAS)** and focused on the conceptual design and analysis of a dipole magnet for a medium-energy beam transport application.

## Engineering focus

- Accelerator magnet design
- Electromagnetic modelling
- Beam transport requirements
- Mechanical design considerations
- Engineering trade-offs between performance and manufacturability

The project provided practical experience connecting accelerator physics requirements with mechanical and electromagnetic engineering.
