## Objective

Develop and evaluate a dipole magnet concept for the Medium Energy Beam Transport section of the MedAustron proton-therapy facility.

The design considered both **C6+ ions and protons** at the maximum energy considered for the MEBT section.

## Engineering requirements

The study addressed:

- Bending angle and good-field region
- Magnetic-field quality
- Vacuum-chamber constraints
- Current and voltage limits
- Water-cooling requirements
- Electromagnetic design
- Manufacturing approach
- Capital and operating cost

## Design and simulation

The selected concept uses an **H-shaped yoke**. The study combines analytical calculations with magnetic-field simulation and manual optimisation.

<img src="/images/octaveFEMM.png" alt="FEMM and Octave magnetic simulation" loading="lazy">

Representative design results include a magnetic field of approximately **1.19 T** for the C6+ case and field quality within the specified good-field region.

## Cost and engineering trade-offs

The design study explicitly considered fabrication and lifetime operating costs.

The report estimates approximately **€67k in fixed initial tooling/design costs**, **€101.6k for four magnets**, and an overall production and operating estimate of approximately **€253.4k** under the assumptions described in the report.

These figures are useful examples of how technical design decisions can be evaluated together with manufacturing and lifecycle considerations.
