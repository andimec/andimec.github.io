## Project at a glance

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Application** | High-speed testing of a synchronous reluctance linear motor |
| **Role** | Mechanical design |
| **Scope** | Concept generation, mechanical architecture and design iteration |
| **Key disciplines** | Mechanical design, dynamics, CAD and experimental engineering |

## Objective

Develop a mechanical concept capable of safely supporting high-speed testing of a synchronous reluctance linear motor.

## Design process

Several concepts were evaluated. After multiple iterations, the **pad-slip concept** was selected as the most suitable solution for the intended test conditions.

<img src="/images/conceptualdesignSRLM.png" alt="Conceptual design of synchronous reluctance linear motor test system" loading="lazy">

## Final assembly

The final concept was developed as a complete mechanical assembly for subsequent engineering evaluation.
