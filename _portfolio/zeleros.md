## Project at a glance

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Zeleros Global |
| **Role** | R&D Mechanical Engineer |
| **Period** | 2019–2022 |
| **Main project** | Static test bench for a linear synchronous reluctance motor |
| **Disciplines** | Mechanical design, multiphysics simulation, instrumentation and testing |
| **Tools** | CAD · ANSYS Mechanical · ANSYS Fluent · FEMM · MATLAB/Octave |

## Objective

Develop experimental infrastructure capable of characterizing advanced magnetic propulsion technologies and validating their mechanical and electromagnetic performance.

## Engineering scope

- Conceptual and detailed mechanical design
- Experimental test-bench development
- Precision tooling for load-cell instrumentation
- Structural and thermal analysis
- Magnetic simulation
- Experimental design
- Manufacturing interfaces
- Test preparation and validation

## My contribution

I worked across the full engineering workflow:

**Concept → CAD → Multiphysics simulation → Manufacturing → Instrumentation → Experimental validation**

The work required close interaction between mechanical design, electromagnetic analysis, structural constraints and measurement requirements.

## Static test bench

The developed test infrastructure enabled controlled characterization of the three force components of a linear motor while allowing the translator to reach the relevant operating states.
