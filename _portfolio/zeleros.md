## Overview

At **Zeleros**, I worked as an R&D Mechanical Engineer on experimental infrastructure for advanced magnetic propulsion technologies.

## Engineering work

- Mechanical design of experimental test benches
- Precision tooling and instrumentation
- Design of experiments
- Structural, thermal and magnetic simulation
- Manufacturing interfaces
- Experimental validation

The project developed my experience in taking complex engineering concepts from CAD and simulation into physical test environments.
