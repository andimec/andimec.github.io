## Current focus

My current work at **IFMIF-DONES** includes accelerator injector systems, the LIPAc ECR ion source, accelerator vacuum and safety engineering, experimental validation, instrumentation and control.

In Japan, I am supporting commissioning, maintenance and operation activities for the LIPAc accelerator while contributing to ECR ion-source lifetime studies, magnetic simulations and the assessment of injector control systems and obsolete technologies.

## Engineering approach

**Requirements → Design → Simulation → Manufacturing → Instrumentation → Integration → Testing → Validation**

I enjoy working across the complete engineering cycle, connecting detailed technical development with system-level problems.
