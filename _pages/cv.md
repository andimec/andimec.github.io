# Professional Profile

**Mechanical & Systems Engineer** specializing in **vacuum systems, accelerator technology, fusion engineering, safety and experimental R&D**.

My experience combines mechanical design, multiphysics simulation, vacuum technology, instrumentation, controls, experimental testing and system integration. I have worked in multidisciplinary and international environments spanning advanced transportation, accelerator technology and fusion research.
